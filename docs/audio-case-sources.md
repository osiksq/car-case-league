# Case and interface audio sources

The case effects use stereo mixes made from recorded Foley and musical samples. The wheel carries a 3.8-second recorded sweep while short typewriter transients mark the card crossings. The sweep resumes at the animation's foreground time after hiding the tab. A short stop cue ends within the 330 ms wheel hold before the rarity reveal begins. The original volume settings and effects bus apply to the complete sequence.

The case cues were trimmed and filtered, then layered with stereo placement, short reflections, level matching and fades as appropriate. Some layers were repitched or reversed. They are encoded as 44.1 kHz MP3. The source inputs are the official highest-quality Freesound previews and the earlier edits of the OpenGameArt originals. CC0 permits commercial distribution, and CC BY 4.0 permits commercial distribution with attribution and identification of edits. The runtime credits list each CC BY source and its adaptations.
